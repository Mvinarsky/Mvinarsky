## Financial-Market Innovation & Intellectual Property

### Early Perception Trading Patent | 1999–2002

In 1999, I developed an early concept for electronically trading nontraditional, information-responsive instruments reflecting changing market perceptions.

I filed a U.S. utility patent application in February 2000 and was awarded U.S. Patent No. 6,390,472 in May 2002, titled Pseudo-commodities interactive futures trading game.

The invention explored exchange-style trading, participant sentiment, and information-driven valuation well before today's widespread commercial interest in prediction markets.

### Perception PX™ | Founder & Platform Developer

Building upon this early intellectual property and decades of market observations, I am developing Perception PX, a proposed financial exchange centered on the continuous evaluation and trading of evolving perceptions.

Unlike conventional binary prediction markets, Perception PX is designed around directional, nonbinary propositions whose perceived value changes as new information becomes available.

Development includes exchange architecture, trading simulations, market-participation mechanisms, and intellectual property strategy.
